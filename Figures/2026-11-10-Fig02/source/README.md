# Reference-image provenance

The SLM hologram (`slm_hologram.png`) and CCD recorded-intensity image (`ccd_intensity.png`) were extracted from the author-supplied manuscript Figure 6 associated with https://doi.org/10.1103/6ftp-svh8. The project author confirmed permission to include these two images on 5 November 2026. They are reference imagery, not patterns or data produced by Blender.

These extracted images and their appearances in example renders retain their existing rights; they are not relicensed under the library MIT or CC BY 4.0 notices. The full paper PDF is not distributed. The article schematic inspired the later Blender project; no Blender models were used in or supplied with the article. This permission does not cover the experimental CAD models.
